Oxide Computer builds rack-scale hardware as a single composable system
rather than a shelf of parts that happen to live together. The rack in
this site's artwork is theirs.

That approach is the same instinct Angelic Infrastructure works from: the
value is in the whole, and the whole has to be designed rather than
assembled.
