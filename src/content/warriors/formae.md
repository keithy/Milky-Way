Formae is the infrastructure-as-code platform Angelic Infrastructure builds
on. Its reason for being is the one that matters most in this line of
work: a declaration that is wrong should fail where it was written, not
where it was applied.

The tooling around it — the agent, the MCP server, the skills that teach an
assistant to drive it — exists so that the discipline is available
without ceremony.
