Cinzel was the wrong face. It is inscriptional and medieval, and the
inscription it imitates belongs to a different building than this one.
So: what else could carry the display work — something with a technical
character, or something celestial?

Below is a range, each rendered at the size the headings actually use.
Every sample is set on the same ground, with the same gold underline, so
the only thing that changes is the letterform.

## The pairs

The faces above are candidates for the headings. They work against the
technical layer, which stays Space Grotesk and JetBrains Mono either way.

**Cormorant Garamond + Space Grotesk** — an almost weightless serif over a
grotesque and a monospace. Heaven over machinery.

**Marcellus + JetBrains Mono** — carved capitals and machine text.

**Spectral + Space Grotesk** — the middle path, a serif that is not too
inscriptional and not too geometric.

**Sora + JetBrains Mono** — everything engineered. No celestial register
at all.

**13 Misa + JetBrains Mono** — the most mechanical of the set, and the
strangest. Square letterforms with hard diagonal cuts, which either read
as machinery or as a videogame.

**Oxanium + Space Grotesk** — machined rather than engraved. The closest
thing on this list to what the site actually sounds like.

## What to look at

Turn the light and dark themes over and read the samples again. The
heavenly faces depend on the ground they sit on — Cormorant in
particular loses too much of itself on the dark theme.

The gold underline is on every sample, because that is what the headings
carry.
