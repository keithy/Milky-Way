A senior platform IC at FTE cost, against a fractional one for a
six-month outcome.

The customer's question is not "is your technology good". It is "what
does it cost me not to hire". That reframing matters, because the
comparison is usually made against the salary rather than against the
cost of not having the capability at all.

A hire is a year of commitment bought in the hope of a year of output.
A fractional engagement is a specific outcome, bought for exactly as
long as it takes to reach it. The arithmetic favours the second whenever
the outcome is bounded — and platform work is usually bounded, because
there is a moment at which the platform is good enough.
